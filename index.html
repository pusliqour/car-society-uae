// Convoy rules

Public roads, road rules.

  • Speed limits apply the whole way.
  • Stay behind the lead car and ahead of the sweep car. No overtaking inside the convoy.
  • Arrive with a full tank, 15 minutes before the start.
  • Valid licence, registration and insurance. Your car, your responsibility.
  • No revving or burnouts at meet points.

// Photos

Get your car featured.

We post cars from around the UAE on the CSAE Instagram. Tell us about yours, send the shots on WhatsApp, and we credit you when it goes up.

// What gets featured
  • The whole car in frame, shot in good light.
  • Original files, not screenshots. No watermarks or heavy filters.
  • Up to five photos per car, portrait or square.

// What we list
  • Events in the UAE that are open to the public or open to sign-ups.
  • Held at a proper venue, or on public roads at legal speeds. No street racing and no takeovers.
  • A named organiser we can reach.
